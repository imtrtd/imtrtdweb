PULSEBOX

An 8-bit chiptune studio in the browser, built like a handheld. Four NES-style channels: lead and harmony pulse, triangle bass, drums. Pick a note or drum, tap or drag across sixteen steps.

Loop one bar or play all four as a song. Duty, level and mute shape the tone. Night Market comes loaded, with Boss Door, River and a blank page. Edits stay on the device. Space plays.

The later build speaks Russian. The pixel font went, it had no Cyrillic. So did the screw frame. Flat panel, same sound.
